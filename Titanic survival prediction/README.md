TITANIC SURVIVAL PREDICTION 


The Titanic Survival Prediction project is a data science effort that aims to estimate the chance of survival for passengers aboard the RMS Titanic based on factors such as passenger class, gender, age, and family links. Data preparation, exploratory data analysis, and the use of machine learning models are all part of the project. The research aims to give insights into which characteristics had the biggest influence on a passenger's survival prospects during the Titanic's tragic sinking by employing classification methods such as Random Forest and Logistic Regression. It gives a complete examination of this historical event through visualizations and model assessments, as well as a great presentation of data science tools for predictive modeling in a real-world setting.
